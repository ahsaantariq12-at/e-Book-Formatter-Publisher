About the studio

One person, every stage of the book.

Bind & Bloom is a one‑studio formatting practice — no outsourcing, no revolving team. The same hands that clean up your manuscript are the ones that build your EPUB, lay out your paperback, and calculate your hardcover spine width.

That means fewer handoffs, fewer inconsistencies between formats, and someone who actually knows your book by the time it's ready to publish.

02 — Process

Five chapters, one finished book.

The same route every manuscript takes on my desk, whether it ends as an ebook, a paperback, or all four formats at once.

Chapter 01

Manuscript Intake

You send the draft as‑is — Word, Google Docs, even a messy PDF. I read it fully, flag structural issues, and confirm trim size, formats, and genre conventions before design starts.

01
02
Chapter 02

Interior Typesetting

Chapter styles, running heads, drop caps, front and back matter go in. You get a styled sample of the opening chapter to approve before the full interior is built.

Chapter 03

Ebook Conversion

The approved interior becomes a validated EPUB3 and a Kindle‑ready MOBI/KPF, tested on real e‑readers and apps so the reflow, TOC, and fonts hold up everywhere.

03
04
Chapter 04

Print‑Ready Files

Paperback and hardcover interiors are built to your printer's exact specs — bleed, gutter, spine width — with cover wraps generated to match final page count.

Chapter 05

Final Proof & Delivery

One last QA pass across every file, a round of your revisions, then delivery of publish‑ready files for KDP, IngramSpark, Draft2Digital, or wherever your book is headed.
